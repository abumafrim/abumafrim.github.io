MafrimDocs

A PDF viewer, editor and annotator that works without a network.

Read, annotate, fill in forms, run text recognition on scans, reorganise pages, encrypt, and sign with a certificate — all on your own machine. It has no account, no telemetry and no analytics, and your documents are never sent anywhere.

It reaches the network only when you ask it to, and only for three things: downloading a language for text recognition, requesting a trusted timestamp when you sign a document, and checking whether a newer version exists. None of those sends a document.

What it does

  • Read and annotate — highlight, comment, draw, and reply to comments. Annotations are written as real PDF annotations, so Adobe and Preview show them properly.
  • Forms — fill them in, and choose whether to finish a form or pass it on still fillable.
  • Text recognition — read the text in a scan, in any of 38 languages. Languages download once, then work offline.
  • Pages — reorder, rotate, delete, merge, split, and insert pages from other files.
  • Signing — sign with a certificate, optionally with a trusted timestamp so the date does not rest on your own computer’s clock. Signing adds to a document rather than replacing it, so an earlier signature stays valid.
  • Encryption — AES-256, with the permissions actually applied.
