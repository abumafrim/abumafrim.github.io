**AfriSenti** and **NaijaSenti** are sentiment-analysis benchmarks for African and Nigerian languages, and the basis of the **AfriSenti-SemEval 2023** shared task. [AfriSenti (EMNLP 2023)](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.862/) · [NaijaSenti (LREC 2022)](https://aclanthology.org/2022.lrec-1.63/).
