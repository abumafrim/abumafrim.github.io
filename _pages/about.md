I build open datasets, models, and benchmarks that bring Natural Language Processing — machine translation, speech, and sentiment, emotion & safety analysis — to African and other low-resource languages.

I am a Research Associate at the University of Pretoria's Data Science for Social Impact Research Group and Principal Investigator of **AfriGemma** (*Advancing African Language NLP for Research, Healthcare, and Societal Benefit*). I hold a PhD from Bayero University Kano, where I worked on making better use of monolingual data — through domain awareness, quality estimation, and transfer learning — for machine translation in low-resource languages.

My work covers LLM building, machine translation, sentiment and emotion analysis, semantic relatedness, hate and offensive speech, named-entity recognition, and sentence alignment. I collaborate with [Masakhane](https://www.masakhane.io/), [HausaNLP](https://github.com/hausanlp), [LITHME](https://lithme.eu/), and the [Open Language Data Initiative](https://oldi.org/), and have co-organised the shared tasks [AfriSenti 2023](https://afrisenti-semeval.github.io/), [SemRel 2024](https://github.com/semantic-textual-relatedness/Semantic_Relatedness_SemEval2024), [BRIGHTER 2025](https://github.com/emotion-analysis-project/SemEval2025-Task11), [Dim-ABSA 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07066), and [POLAR 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.06817). I also teach NLP and mentor students and early-career researchers.
