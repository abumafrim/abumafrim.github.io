Last updated: 11 November 2026

MafrimDocs is a PDF viewer and editor for Android, iPhone, iPad and Mac. It works on your device. It has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not collect, store or share personal information.

This policy applies to MafrimDocs on every platform. The app is made by Idris Abdulmumin.

## Your documents

The PDFs and pictures you open, scan, edit or create stay on your device. MafrimDocs does not upload them, and its maker never receives them.

Work you have not yet saved to a file is kept as a draft inside the app, on your device, so that you can find it under Recent. Deleting the app deletes those drafts.

A document leaves your device only when you send it yourself, for example with Share or by saving it to a cloud folder you have chosen. What happens to it after that is governed by the service you sent it to.

## What the app asks permission for

- **Camera.** Used when you scan a page or add a photo. The pictures go into your document and nowhere else.
- **Photos and files.** Used when you choose a picture or a PDF to open, and when you choose where to save one. The app reads only what you pick.
- **Face, fingerprint or passcode.** Used only if you turn on the app lock. The check is done by your phone. MafrimDocs is told whether it succeeded and never sees your face, your fingerprint or your passcode.

## When the app connects to the internet

MafrimDocs works without a connection. It connects only in these cases, each of which you start yourself. None of them sends a document.

- **Check for Updates.** The app reads a small file on abumafrim.com that gives the latest version number.
- **Extra languages for text recognition.** If you choose a recognition language that is not built in, its data file is downloaded from the jsDelivr network (cdn.jsdelivr.net). Recognition itself then runs on your device.
- **Trusted timestamps.** If you sign a document with a certificate and ask for a timestamp, a fingerprint of the signature, not the document, is sent to the timestamp authority you choose (DigiCert, Sectigo or FreeTSA).
- **Links.** Choosing About the Creator opens abumafrim.com in your browser.

As with any connection, the servers involved can see your IP address. MafrimDocs sends them nothing that identifies you or describes your documents.

## Information collected by the app stores

If you install MafrimDocs from Google Play or the App Store, Google or Apple may collect installation, crash and performance information under their own privacy policies and your device settings. That information is collected by them, not by the app.

## Children

MafrimDocs is a general tool and is not directed at children. It collects no information from anyone, children included.
